# Cover Letter Template

*Bot instructions: this is the template. Replace bracketed sections per role. Apply the user's writing voice on every draft. Keep total length ≤ 250 words. Open with the angle, never with "I am writing to express interest." Active voice throughout. No em-dashes as separators. No "leverage," "robust," "transformative," "drove," "spearheaded." No negative parallelism ("It's not X, it's Y"). No bloated verbs ("serves as," "represents a"). No dead transitions ("Furthermore," "Moreover"). No fake-depth participle phrases ("highlighting…," "underscoring…").*
